SP Drop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

SP Drop is an application that connects with eBay to help users manage information and actions associated with their eBay account.

Information We Access

When you authorize SP Drop to connect to your eBay account, the application may access information permitted by the eBay API permissions you approve.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through eBay is used only to provide the functionality of SP Drop and to perform actions that you have authorized.

Information Sharing

SP Drop does not sell your personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the application's functionality or where required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information accessed through SP Drop from unauthorized access.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or SP Drop, please contact the application owner.
